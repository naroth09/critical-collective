Biography
Writes on
Modern & contemporary art, gender, museums, the archive

An art critic and curator whose writing addresses modern and contemporary art in India, with a sustained interest in gender, iconography and the archive. She founded Critical Collective as a platform for scholarship on South Asian art, and writes here on exhibitions, artists and the histories that frame them.
