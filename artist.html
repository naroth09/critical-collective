Artists / A Ramachandran

A Ramachandran

1935–2024 · Painter · Kerala-born, Delhi-based

A Delhi-based painter who held numerous solo and group exhibitions across India and internationally. He was awarded the Padma Bhushan by the Government of India in 2005. Alongside his painting, he wrote and illustrated children’s books published in India, Japan, England and the USA, which won international recognition.
