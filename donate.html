Support Critical Collective

Art writing that answers to no one but the reader.

For fifteen years Critical Collective has commissioned essays, interviews and archives on art from South Asia — and kept every word of it free to read. No paywall, no sponsor deciding what deserves attention, no algorithm sorting scholarship by clicks.

That independence is expensive and quietly fragile. It is paid for by readers who believe a serious record of this art should exist — and should belong to everyone. If you are one of them, give what you can.
