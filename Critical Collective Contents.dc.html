Art, Media and Law

Open

Brings forth the modern and contemporary life of art and popular culture, placing South Asian visual culture within changes in technology, digital and print cultures, legal regimes of ownership and censorship, and new ways of fashioning identities. Through interviews, reviews and essays it seeks to understand the process of modernity within the visual realm from the 19th century onwards.

Noticeboard

Subscription

A dynamic space covering emergent trends in contemporary art — exhibition reviews in India and abroad, interviews with artists, collectives and curators, and topical book reviews. Noticeboard also platforms smaller practices manifest on social media or outside the leading art circuits of metropolitan cities, acting as a monthly bulletin on presentations of South Asian art at home and abroad.

Art History Project

Subscription

A richly illustrated section of critical writing on art, architectural and religious traditions, and the decorative and folk arts of the subcontinent. Younger art historians bring fresh scholarship to their readings and analyses.

Museums

Subscription

Initiated in 2019, an attempt at building a critical framework on museology in the subcontinent, with a focus on new scholarship on museums of the 21st century. It maps smaller museums across India that break from the mainstream museum framework, addressing trauma, regional interests and local cultural heritage through interviews, reviews and archival material.

Cinema / OTT

Open

Original and archival essays on the evolving cinema industry in India, television and new-age OTT platforms, with interviews with leading directors, writers, actors and other professionals, and sections dedicated to gender and documentary cinema.

Timeline: Landmarks in Indian Photography

Special subscription

Two years of dedicated research compiled into a scrolling, extensively illustrated document on the cultural history of photography in the subcontinent — early practitioners and societies, the expansion of photography as an aesthetic and political medium, and its intersections with cinema and Indian painting. Presented in ten sections across 150 years, available through institutional subscription or a special individual subscription.

Open-access segments

CC Archives

A repository of writing reflecting the discourse on Indian art from 1900 to the present, including criticism by John Berger, K. B. Goel, Herman Goetz, E. B. Havell, Sister Nivedita, Kamaladevi Chattopadhyay, Mulk Raj Anand, Nihar Ranjan Ray, Octavio Paz, Nissim Ezekiel and Geeta Kapur — alongside artists’ perspectives by Rabindranath Tagore, Nandalal Bose, M. F. Husain, F. N. Souza, K. G. Subramanyan, Satyajit Ray, Akbar Padamsee, Bhupen Khakhar, Gulammohammed Sheikh, Sudhir Patwardhan and Nalini Malani.

Artists

A database of the artists who have shaped modern and contemporary Indian art, with biographies, artworks and essays on the practice of over 150 artists.

Artist Conversations

Interviews with artists exploring their practice in depth.

Curators and Exhibitions

Profiles of leading curators, with reports and essays from major art events including the Kochi Biennale, the India Art Fair, the Venice Biennale, Documenta and Manifesta.

Lens Based Practices

A resource on writing about photography, its history and its overlap with other media. This section also covers the CC / Photo South Asia awards for writing on lens based practices.
